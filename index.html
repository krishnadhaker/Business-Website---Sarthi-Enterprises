About Sarthi Enterprises

Built on Direct, On-Ground Industrial Experience

Sarthi Enterprises is a proprietorship led by Mukesh Dhaker, based in Chittorgarh, Rajasthan — a region at the heart of India's cement industry. The company trades and transports raw materials and AFR (Alternative Fuel and Raw Materials) products for cement plants, and separately carries out civil construction work.

With 2+ years in trading and transportation and 3+ years in civil construction, Sarthi Enterprises works directly with material sourcing, supply and delivery — keeping communication and accountability close to the client.
