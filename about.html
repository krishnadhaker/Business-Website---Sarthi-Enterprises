Company Profile

A Direct, Accountable Partner for Cement Plant Requirements

Sarthi Enterprises operates from Chittorgarh, Rajasthan — trading raw materials and AFR products for cement plants, transporting them, and separately delivering civil construction support.

Introduction

Who We Are

Sarthi Enterprises is a proprietorship business owned and operated by Mukesh Dhaker, registered in Chittorgarh, Rajasthan. The company was built around a simple idea: cement plants need dependable material supply and transportation, delivered by someone who understands both the material and the logistics behind it.

Today, Sarthi Enterprises trades and transports cement plant raw materials and AFR (Alternative Fuel and Raw Materials) products, and separately undertakes civil construction and industrial project support.

2+Years in Trading & Transportation
3+Years in Civil Construction
Main Business Activities

Three Areas of Operation

01

Cement Plant Raw Material Trading

Sourcing and supply of gypsum, red ochre, fibre, limestone and fly ash, with transportation support included.

02

AFR Products

Trading and transportation of RDF waste and plastic waste for use as alternative fuel and raw material in cement manufacturing.

03

Civil Construction

Civil and industrial project support built on 3+ years of on-site construction experience.

Vision

To be a dependable, long-term material and logistics partner for cement plants and industrial buyers across Rajasthan.

Mission

To supply the right material, on time, with transportation and communication that buyers can rely on — without unnecessary complexity.

Values

Reliability, transparency, timely service, responsible business practice, and long-term relationships with every client.
