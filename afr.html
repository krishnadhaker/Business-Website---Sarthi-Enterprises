What is AFR?

Alternative Fuel & Raw Materials, Explained

AFR stands for Alternative Fuel and Raw Materials. In cement manufacturing, certain waste-derived materials — when appropriately processed, assessed and handled according to applicable requirements — can be used to supplement conventional fuel and raw material inputs.

Sarthi Enterprises' role in this chain is trading and transportation: sourcing AFR material, coordinating handling, and delivering it to cement plant requirements.
