Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sarthi Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to share information about our products and services and to receive business enquiries. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you submit the "Request a Quote" form, we collect the details you choose to provide: your name, company name, phone number, email address, requirement type, material/product of interest, quantity, and any message you include. We do not require account creation or collect payment information through this website.

WhatsApp & Phone Communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp or phone, your number will be used solely to respond to your enquiry.
